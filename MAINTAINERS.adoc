== Areas of responsibility

Because there is one maintainer, ownership is not divided by area. What *is*
divided is the level of scrutiny a change receives:

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Area | Review standard

| `index.html` — the generator (`generateSpecimen`) and orderings
| Highest. Both panes must consume the identical deterministic voxel set; any
  change that lets the Sculptor and the Gardener drift apart destroys the
  invariant the page exists to demonstrate. The in-page verifier must stay
  green.

| `index.html` — the instruments (ledgers, triangle counts, operation tapes)
| Highest. Instruments report engine state (`InstancedMesh.count`,
  `renderer.info.render.triangles`), never scripted numbers. A readout that
  shows what the script expects rather than what the engine did is the exact
  failure mode the page exists to refute.

| `index.html` — presentation (CSS, copy, camera, chips)
| Normal.

| `.github/workflows/`
| High. The org pins actions transitively; see the header comment in
  `pages.yml` before touching any `uses:` line.

| Docs, `.machine_readable/`
| Normal.
|===

== Becoming a maintainer

There is no formal ladder yet, which is the honest position for a project this
size. In practice: contribute substantively, demonstrate that you understand
why the invariant and the instruments exist rather than merely that they
exist, and ask.
