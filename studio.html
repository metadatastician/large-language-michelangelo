    What this block can and cannot say

    The block is 27 cells wide and deep by 40 tall — 29,160 in all. Recognisable silhouettes work: aircraft, animals, chairs, towers, instruments. Faces, lettering, named characters and fine texture do not, at any prompt, because there are not enough cells to carry them. Style survives as palette and proportion, not as line or brushwork.

    The recipe, not the prompt, is this page’s interface. A recipe is a few hundred bytes of JSON naming capsules, ellipsoids and boxes in a normalised cube; the page fits it to the block and rasterises it the same way every time. That is why a revision can be counted rather than merely observed, and why the whole page keeps working when no model is available.

    The verifier below re-derives disjointness, completeness and convergence from the live buffers after every build. It proves the partition, never the likeness: a recipe can be wrong about the subject and still be right about the invariant. Asking for a form in words is paid for by whoever is viewing, and asks consent the first time.
